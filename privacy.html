Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

"bookerfan59 upload" (the "App") is a personal tool used only by the owner of the @bookerfan59 TikTok account to upload that owner's own videos.

What the App accesses

  • Basic TikTok profile information (open ID, display name, avatar), used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Permission to upload or post videos to the connected account.

What the App stores

Only the access and refresh tokens TikTok issues, kept in private, encrypted configuration so uploads can run on a schedule. The App does not read, collect, sell or share any other user data, viewer data, messages or analytics.

Third parties

Data is exchanged only with TikTok, through TikTok's official APIs. Nothing is shared with advertisers or other third parties.

Deleting your data

Revoke the App in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services). The stored tokens then stop working and are deleted.

Questions: via the @bookerfan59 TikTok account.
